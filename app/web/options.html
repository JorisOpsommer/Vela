Prices come from the broker's indicative options feed: derived from the exchange quotes, with trades delayed 15 minutes. Open interest is the broker's daily figure, published the morning after. Dealer gamma assumes dealers are long the calls and short the puts customers trade — a common estimate of hedging pressure, not a measurement. Greeks missing from the feed are filled in with a Black-Scholes model. Margin figures are the broker's own requirements; exchange and option margin are not included. Nothing here is investment advice.
